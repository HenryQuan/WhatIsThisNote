# WhatIsThisNote 0.1

The first real release. Learn to read music by dragging a note around the
staff: ledger lines, key signatures and note names update live, with a mini
piano keyboard you can listen to and play back.

## Note

- Drag or tap the note to change its pitch; it snaps to the nearest staff
  position with a smooth snap animation.
- Automatic ledger lines above and below the staff.
- Treble, bass and alto clefs.
- All 15 major and 15 minor key signatures, drawn on the staff.
- Note names in scientific pitch (C, D, E...), fixed-do solfege (Do, Re, Mi...)
  and numbered notation (jianpu, 1 = Do ... 7 = Ti).
- Optional enharmonic twin spelling (F♯ ≈ G♭).
- Mini one-octave piano keyboard showing which key the note is on.
- Hear the note, or the current chord, with a built-in synthesizer (no audio
  assets).
- Display preferences: naming system, on-staff name label and enharmonic twin,
  saved between sessions.

### Scale highlight (add-on)

- Major, natural/harmonic/melodic minor, major/minor pentatonic and blues,
  tinted on the piano keyboard with the note's scale degree.

### Chord lab (add-on)

- Diatonic triads through thirteenths (sixths, sevenths, ninths, elevenths,
  thirteenths), shown on the staff and piano with their chord symbol and Roman
  numeral.
- Root, 1st, 2nd and 3rd inversions with slash-bass names.
- Tappable progressions (pop, doo-wop, jazz ii-V-I, canon, Andalusian and
  more), with a play button to hear the selected progression as a sequence.

## Practice

- **Practice**: a name-the-note quiz with four choices, a running score, a
  streak and a best streak.
- **Read & play**: a generated single-voice phrase to read off the staff and
  play back on the on-screen keyboard.
- **Guided theory path**: short lessons on the staff's lines and spaces, the
  bass clef and sharps/flats. Each step explains a little, then asks you to
  drag the note to a target drawn as a hollow notehead.

## Metronome

- 40-208 BPM with traditional tempo names, ±1/±10 controls and 2/4 through
  6/4 bars, with a live beat indicator.
- Register finder: walk through note and octave zone to find your range, with
  play, loop and sweep.

## Chords

- Tap the staff to add notes, then drag them or nudge with the arrows.
- Add the next third above or below, and apply sharps or flats to the selected
  note.
- Play the built chord.
- Possible-chords list across all twelve roots: chord symbol, spelled note
  names, inversion/bass role, and the added or omitted tones for the closest
  shape.
- Mini keyboard showing the chord tones (with the upper C handled correctly).
- Clear all to start over.

## About

- Version, display and theme settings in one place.
- Links to the GitHub repository, email and the web version.
- Open-source licenses, including the bundled Bravura music font (SIL OFL).

## General

- Simple, clean Material 3 UI with light, dark and system themes.
- Responsive layout: line spacing scales with the screen and tablets use a
  side-by-side layout.
- Stable layout: the staff fills the space the controls leave and the readout
  never changes the panel's height, so the view does not jump while dragging.
- First-run coach mark explaining the drag and tap gestures, shown once and
  remembered.
- Accessibility: the staff and note are labelled for screen readers (the
  answer stays hidden in practice), arrow keys nudge the note, and "reduce
  motion" makes the snap instant.
- Runs on Android, iOS, web, Windows, macOS and Linux; clefs and noteheads are
  drawn with the bundled Bravura music font.

## Known limits

- The chord lab builds chords on seven-note scales only; it falls back to the
  key's major or natural minor scale and shows no chord for chromatic notes.
- The guided path draws sharps and flats from key signatures only, so it does
  not yet show accidentals outside the key.
